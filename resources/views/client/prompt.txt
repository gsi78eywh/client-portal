1. Purpose and Scope

This document defines the first client-facing commercial ORDO experience: account creation, login, password reset, immediate system access, 30-day free access, progressive account completion and verification, module visibility, JK&C client-service areas, and a centralized Settings architecture.

V1 RULE

Phase 1 is primarily visual and navigational. Pages, menus, states, buttons, forms, tabs, locks, progress indicators, and sample dashboards must work as a clickable mockup. Actual database-driven rules may be simulated, but the UI and routing must be ready for Phase 2 wiring.

5. Client Entry Journey

1. User opens ORDO and chooses Log In or Create Account.

2. New user completes short progressive registration screens.

3. Account is created and the 30-day free-access period begins immediately.

4. User enters Town Hall without waiting for full account verification.

5. Town Hall shows account setup progress, verification status, free-access status, days remaining, and important JK&C announcements.

6. During the first 30 days, all six Business modules are available for exploration.

7. Account Profile and Account Verification may be completed during the 30-day period.

8. Before the free period ends, the user is prompted to choose up to three Business modules to retain on the Free Plan.

9. After 30 days, access may become limited depending on verification/subscription state. Existing data is retained and never deleted simply because access changes.

7. Town Hall

Town Hall is the client's main landing page and should feel immediately useful even while most data is still static.

Welcome / Account Status: Account name, current plan, verification status, 30-day status, days remaining.

Account Setup Progress: Account Created, Contact Confirmed, Account Profile, Account Verification with a progress bar and Continue Setup action.

Action Required: Items requiring client attention: setup, verification, required documents, approvals, support responses, or billing actions.

Business Overview: Compact cards for subscribed/trial modules using sample data.

Announcements: Latest important JK&C announcements; full archive remains under JK&C > Announcements.

Upcoming: Deadlines, renewals, meetings, engagement milestones, billing dates.

Recent Activity: Recent records, transmittals, compliance activity, JK&C service activity.

8. Free Access, Module Selection and Limits
8.1 First 30 Days

30-DAY FREE ACCESS

All six Business modules are available during the initial free period: Entity & Governance, Compliance, Finance, Human Capital, Records, and Transmittals.

· Show the remaining days in Town Hall and Subscriptions.

· Use Trial status on modules during this period.

· The mockup should make all six modules clickable.

· Static sample data should demonstrate how each module could look when active.

8.2 Free Plan After Trial

The Free Plan allows the client to select up to three Business modules.

· Selected modules remain visible as Free/Active.

· Unselected modules remain visible but display Locked / Upgrade Required.

· Do not hide locked modules completely; the client should still see the available ORDO ecosystem.

· Detailed child-feature limits per module will be defined later.

8.3 Three Separate Entitlement Layers

Records 76/100; Users 1/1; Storage 320 MB/500 MB

8.4 After 30 Days - Limited Access

· Do not delete the account or stored records.

· Existing records remain stored.

· Some records/features/modules may become restricted or read-only according to future rules.

· Town Hall, JK&C Announcements, Engagements, Subscriptions, Support, Billing, Settings, and Account Verification must remain reachable.

· Show a clear Account Verification Required / Limited Access notice with a Continue Verification action.
